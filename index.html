htmx calendar from a server-rendered table

The server renders a bookings table and an on-call rota as plain tables. Lattice turns the first into a calendar bound to its grid and the second into a calendar of its own, with no mount code, and htmx handles the swaps, the out-of-band update and the save. A stand-in server answers htmx's requests in your browser, so nothing leaves the page.

Saved by the server

    Try the five steps

    1. Built on load. The bookings were a server-rendered table grid and the first calendar is bound to it; the on-call rota was a plain table inside the second calendar, which replaced it on load.
    2. Move an event. Drag a booking in the first calendar, or a shift in the second. lattice:calendar-change is posted with hx-post and the server confirms below.
    3. Out of band. Press "Server moves one booking" or "Server moves one shift". One row comes back out of band and its card moves a day, in the grid and the calendar.
    4. Swap. Pick a team, or the Week view. hx-get replaces the whole view and both calendars are rebuilt from the new tables.
    5. Back. Change the team and the view, then press the browser Back button. The previous team and view return, with both calendars populated.
